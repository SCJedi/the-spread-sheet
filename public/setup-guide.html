Step 1 of 9

Make a free GitHub account

Go to github.com/signup and make an account. Then open the email GitHub sends you and verify your email.

Open github.com/signup (opens in a new tab)
?Why?

GitHub is where your copy of the app's code lives. Think of it as a free online folder for the app.

!Stuck?
  • Already have a GitHub account? Use it and skip to step 2.
  • No email yet? Check your spam or junk folder, then wait a minute.
2

Step 2 of 9

Make a free Cloudflare account

Go to dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up and make an account. Then verify your email. No credit card is needed for the free plan.

Open the Cloudflare sign-up (opens in a new tab)
?Why?

Cloudflare is the computer on the internet that runs your site, so your friends can open it from anywhere. The free plan is enough.

!Stuck?
  • Asked for a card? You don't need one for the free plan.
  • No email yet? Check your spam or junk folder, then wait a minute.
3

Step 3 of 9

Press the big orange button

Press Deploy to Cloudflare. It opens Cloudflare in a new tab.

Deploy to Cloudflare (opens in a new tab)

It's the same button that's in the project's README.

?Why?

This button tells Cloudflare which app to copy. It does the technical setup for you in one go.

!Stuck?
  • It says the repository can't be found? The repository must be public, and the link must be exactly https://github.com/SCJedi/the-spread-sheet.
4

Step 4 of 9

Sign in and connect GitHub

Sign in when Cloudflare asks, then connect GitHub. Allow Cloudflare to create a repository in your GitHub account.

?Why?

A repository is a project folder on GitHub. Cloudflare copies the app into a new one in your account, so the copy is yours.

!Stuck?
  • Use the GitHub account you made in step 1 when it asks you to sign in.
  • It says the repository can't be found? Go back to step 3 and press the button on this page again.
5

Step 5 of 9

Keep the defaults, press Create

Leave the names as they are and press Create and deploy.

?Why?

The boxes name your project, your Worker (the part that runs the site) and your database (where picks and scores are saved). The defaults work, and Cloudflare makes the database for you.

!Stuck?
  • The build failed? Open the deployment in the Cloudflare dashboard and press Retry deployment. If it fails twice, open an issue on the GitHub repo.
6

Step 6 of 9

Wait 1 to 3 minutes

Wait while it builds. When it's done, Cloudflare shows your site's address, like https://the-spread-sheet.<your-name>.workers.dev.

The first time, Cloudflare may ask you to pick your <your-name> part (your workers.dev subdomain).

?Why?

Cloudflare is building your site and putting it on the internet. The address is how everyone finds it, so save it somewhere.

!Stuck?
  • Asked for a workers.dev subdomain? Pick any short name. It becomes part of your site address.
  • The build failed? Open the deployment in the Cloudflare dashboard and press Retry deployment. If it fails twice, open an issue on the GitHub repo.
7

Step 7 of 9

Open it. You're the commissioner!

Open your address. You're the first one there, so you become the commissioner. Pick your display name and a 4 to 8 digit PIN.

?Why?

The commissioner runs the pool. Your PIN is like a short password, so pick one you'll remember and keep it to yourself.

!Stuck?
  • It says "No games loaded yet"? Wait one minute and refresh. The schedule loads on the first visit.
  • Five wrong PINs lock that name for 15 minutes.
  • Forgot your PIN later? See If you forget your PIN.
8

Step 8 of 9

Invite everyone

Go to Admin → Players and copy the invite link. Text it to your group.

They open it, pick a name and a PIN, and they're in.

?Why?

The invite link has your pool's join code built in, so your friends don't have to type anything extra.

!Stuck?
  • A player forgot their PIN? You reset it in Admin → Players → New PIN → Save.
  • Five wrong PINs lock that name for 15 minutes.
9

Step 9 of 9 · Optional

Make it yours (optional)

If you like, open Admin → Settings for the pool name, theme and hide picks until kickoff, and Admin → Data source for where scores come from.

You can skip this. The pool works with the defaults.

?Why?

Settings make the pool feel like your group's. The data source is where the schedule and scores come from. Automatic is the default, so you don't have to pick.

!Stuck?
  • Scores stopped updating? The automatic feed is unofficial. Switch to Manual or your own feed in Admin → Data source.

You did it!

Your pool is live. Text the invite link to your group and start picking.

Extra

Commissioner tips

Every week, nothing is required. The current week appears on its own (the automatic source rolls over around Wednesday).

  • Want picks to open sooner? Admin → This week → Load next week early.
  • Enter picks for people who text you their picks. It works after kickoff, and everything goes in the change log.
  • Post a board message, choose the tiebreak game, fix a result with a result override, or give a player a new PIN.

Where scores come from

  • Automatic (the default): ESPN's public NFL scoreboard. Free, no key needed. It's unofficial, so it could change or stop.
  • Your own feed: any https address that returns the format in FEED.md, with an optional API key. Test connection checks it first.
  • Manual: no feed. Add the week and its games, then type final scores.

Extra

If you forget your PIN

A player forgot their PIN? The commissioner gives them a new one: Admin → Players → New PIN → Save.

The commissioner forgot theirs? Do this:

  1. Open dash.cloudflare.com → Workers & Pages → your project (the-spread-sheet) → Settings → Variables and Secrets → Add.
  2. Type RECOVERY_PIN as the name and a new 4 to 8 digit PIN as the value. Save and deploy.
  3. Open your site, Log in, type your commissioner name and that new PIN. It becomes your PIN.
  4. Go back and delete the RECOVERY_PIN variable. The site shows a reminder until you do.
