========================================================================
                          THE SPREAD SHEET
              Your picks sheet, minus the spreadsheet.
========================================================================

Hi! This is a free website for picking games with friends and family.

Everyone can look at the picks board. You do not need to log in to
look. To make your own picks, you log in with a name and a secret
number called a PIN. A PIN is 4 to 8 numbers.

Picks lock when each game starts. Scores and winners fill in by
themselves. No money. No betting. Just for fun.

It is free. It runs on a free Cloudflare account. GitHub is free too.

You can do this. Take your time. Ask a grown-up to help if you like.
Do one step, then the next.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO SET IT UP (about 10 minutes)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1. Make a free GitHub account.

   Go to this address:
   https://github.com/signup

   Then check your email and verify it.


STEP 2. Make a free Cloudflare account.

   Go to this address:
   https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up

   Check your email and verify it.
   You do not need a credit card for the free plan.


STEP 3. Click the "Deploy to Cloudflare" button.

   The button is near the top of the project page:
   https://github.com/SCJedi/the-spread-sheet


STEP 4. Sign in and connect GitHub.

   Cloudflare will ask you to sign in and connect GitHub.
   Say yes. Let Cloudflare make a repository in your GitHub account.
   It copies the project there for you.


STEP 5. Keep the defaults on the setup page.

   Do not change the project name, Worker name or database name.
   Press the button that says: Create and deploy

   Cloudflare makes the database for you.


STEP 6. Wait about 1 to 3 minutes.

   It is building your site. When it is done, Cloudflare shows
   your site's address. It looks like this:

   https://the-spread-sheet.<your-name>.workers.dev

   The first time, Cloudflare may ask you to pick the <your-name>
   part. This is called your workers.dev subdomain. Pick any short
   name.


STEP 7. Open your address.

   You are the first one there, so you become the commissioner.
   The commissioner is the boss of the pool.
   Pick your display name and a PIN (4 to 8 numbers).
   Write your PIN down somewhere safe.


STEP 8. Invite everyone.

   Go to Admin, then Players. Copy the invite link.
   The join code is already inside it.
   Text the link to your group.
   They open it, pick a name and a PIN, and they are in.


STEP 9. (You can skip this.)

   Go to Admin, then Settings, to set the pool name, the theme, and
   whether picks stay hidden until kickoff.
   Go to Admin, then Data source, to choose where scores come from.


ALL DONE!

   The schedule, scores and winners update by themselves every
   10 minutes. You do not have to do anything else.

   Want pictures? Your new site has a picture guide at:
   https://the-spread-sheet.<your-name>.workers.dev/setup-guide.html


------------------------------------------------------------------------
EVERY WEEK
------------------------------------------------------------------------

You do not have to do anything. The new week shows up by itself,
around Wednesday.

Want picks to open sooner? Go to Admin, then This week, then press
"Load next week early".

If a friend texts you their picks, you can type them in for them.
Go to Admin, then Enter picks.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMEONE FORGETS THEIR PIN
------------------------------------------------------------------------

A player forgot their PIN:

   The commissioner fixes it. Go to Admin, then Players.
   Press New PIN, type a new one, and press Save.

The commissioner forgot their PIN:

   That's okay. Here is how to get back in.

   1. Go to https://dash.cloudflare.com
      Click Workers & Pages.
      Click your project. It is called the-spread-sheet.
      Click Settings.
      Click Variables and Secrets.
      Click Add.

   2. For the name, type:   RECOVERY_PIN
      For the value, type a new PIN (4 to 8 numbers).
      Save and deploy.

   3. Open your site. Press Log in.
      Type your commissioner name and that new PIN.
      Now that is your PIN.

   4. Go back to Cloudflare and delete RECOVERY_PIN.
      Your site will remind you until you do.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
------------------------------------------------------------------------

It says "No games loaded yet" right after setup.
   Wait one minute. Then refresh the page.

The button says it can't find the repository.
   The project must be public. Check the address is exactly:
   https://github.com/SCJedi/the-spread-sheet

Cloudflare asks for a workers.dev subdomain.
   Pick any short name. It becomes part of your site address.

The build failed.
   Open the deployment in the Cloudflare dashboard.
   Press "Retry deployment".
   If it fails two times, open an issue on the GitHub project page.

Someone typed the wrong PIN five times.
   That name is locked for 15 minutes. Then they can try again.

The scores stopped updating.
   The automatic scores are not official, so they can stop.
   Go to Admin, then Data source. Choose Manual, or your own feed.
